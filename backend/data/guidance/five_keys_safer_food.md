---
title: Five keys to safer food
category: food_safety
origin: Paraphrased summary of WHO "Five Keys to Safer Food" (2006)
---

## Keep clean

Wash your hands with soap and water before handling food and often while cooking, and after
using the toilet, changing nappies or touching raw meat, chicken or fish. Wash and sanitise
chopping boards, knives and kitchen surfaces after contact with raw food. Keep insects, pests
and pets away from food and the kitchen. Most micro-organisms do not make food look or smell
different, so clean habits matter even when food seems fine.

## Separate raw and cooked food

Keep raw meat, chicken and fish away from other foods, both in the shopping bag and in the
fridge. Store raw meat in covered containers on the lowest shelf so its juices cannot drip onto
cooked food, curd or cut fruit. Use a separate knife and chopping board for raw meat, or wash
them well with hot soapy water before using them for anything else. Never put cooked food back
on a plate that held raw meat.

## Cook thoroughly

Cook food thoroughly, especially meat, chicken, eggs and seafood. Bring soups, dals and curries
to a boil. For meat and chicken, make sure the juices are clear, not pink; a food thermometer
should read at least 70 °C in the thickest part. Reheat cooked food until it is steaming hot
all the way through, not just warm on the surface.

## Keep food at safe temperatures

Bacteria multiply quickly between 5 °C and 60 °C, the "danger zone". Do not leave cooked food
at room temperature for more than 2 hours. Refrigerate cooked and perishable food promptly,
preferably below 5 °C. Keep hot food piping hot (above 60 °C) until it is served. Do not store
food too long even in the fridge, and do not thaw frozen food at room temperature.

## Use safe water and raw materials

Use safe, clean water for drinking and cooking, or treat it (for example by boiling). Choose
fresh, wholesome foods and processed foods such as pasteurised milk. Wash fruits and vegetables
under clean running water, especially if they are eaten raw. Do not use food beyond its expiry
date.
